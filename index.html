A/B Test Log — Discount Promo on Repeat Purchase

Hypothesis

H₀: A 10% discount promo has no effect on repeat-purchase rate.
H₁: A 10% discount promo increases repeat-purchase rate.

Sample Balance

  • t = −0.195, p = 0.845 on avg_order_value pre-treatment — groups are balanced.

Result

  • Treatment repeat rate: 0.6905
  • Control repeat rate: 0.6480
  • Absolute lift: +4.26 percentage points
  • z = 2.482, one-sided p = 0.0065

Business Recommendation

✅ SHIP — the 4.26 pp lift is statistically significant (p = 0.0065). Modeled incremental revenue (R$ 201,746) exceeds the modeled discount cost (R$ 164,349), yielding positive ROI.
